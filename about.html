💡 Our Mission & Craft

Empowering Pet Parents with Private, Reliable Care Tools

We build tools designed to work when it matters most — at the veterinary emergency room, during kennel drop-offs, and without relying on internet connectivity.

Why We Created PetKeeper

As pet parents ourselves, we noticed a persistent problem with most existing pet apps: they require constant high-speed internet, bombard users with intrusive third-party banner ads, and lock critical medical histories behind clunky cloud-only portals.

When you are at a veterinary clinic with weak basement cell reception, or traveling with your pet outdoors, you need your pet's vaccination batch numbers, allergy records, and emergency contacts immediately. PetKeeper was engineered from the ground up to be offline-first, private, and lightning fast.

Our Philosophy: Your pet's medical data belongs to you. We do not sell user data, run advertising trackers, or restrict your access to offline records.

Core Engineering Principles

1. Offline Independence with SQLite

Every pet profile, reminder, expense record, and medical event is saved locally on your Android device in a high-speed SQLite database with C++ bindings. The app opens instantly and performs all searches, filtering, and updates without network lag.

2. Multi-Tenant Cloud Isolation

When you choose to synchronize across devices, your structured data is synced to Cloud Firestore and your medical documents/photos are encrypted and stored in private Cloudflare R2 vaults. Each user's data is isolated strictly by their Firebase Auth UID with production security rules.

3. Zero Advertising & Zero Telemetry

PetKeeper contains no AdMob, no third-party behavioral trackers, and no telemetry SDKs. We focus purely on software craftsmanship, battery efficiency, and privacy.

4. Emergency & Caregiver Readiness

From generating an instant Vet Summary PDF to exporting caregiver handoff checklists for pet sitters, PetKeeper simplifies communication so your pet always receives optimal care.
