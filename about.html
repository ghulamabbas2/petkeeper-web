💡 Our Mission & Craft

Built for the Vet Waiting Room, Not the Boardroom

We build tools designed to work when it matters most — at the emergency clinic, during kennel drop-off, and without relying on a signal bar.

Why We Created PetKeeper

As pet parents ourselves, we ran into the same problem with every existing app: they need constant high-speed internet and bury critical records behind slow cloud-only portals.

When you're in a clinic basement with no signal, or your dog needs a med dosed at 3am, you need the vaccination lot number, the allergy list, and the emergency contact right now — not after a loading spinner. PetKeeper is engineered offline-first, private, and fast, from the database up.

Our philosophy: your pet's medical data belongs to you. We don't sell it, and we never gate your own records behind a subscription wall.

Core Engineering Principles

1. Offline Independence, by Default

Every pet profile, reminder, expense, and medical event is saved directly on your device first, built for speed from the ground up. Search, filtering, and edits happen instantly — no network round-trip, ever, for anything you've already saved.

2. Strict Multi-Tenant Isolation

When you turn on sync, your records go to our secure cloud database and your photos and documents go to a private, encrypted cloud vault — each one locked to your account and enforced at the server level, not just inside the app.

3. No Behavioral Trackers

PetKeeper contains no behavioral trackers and no analytics SDKs of any kind. We never watch what you do inside the app, and we never sell what's in it.

4. Ready for the Moments That Matter

From an instant zero-network emergency card to a one-tap vet summary PDF and a sitter handoff sheet, every export exists because a real pet parent needed it in a real hurry.
