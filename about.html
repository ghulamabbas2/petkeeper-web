💡 Our Mission & Craft

Built for the Vet Waiting Room, Not the Ad Dashboard

We build tools designed to work when it matters most — at the emergency clinic, during kennel drop-off, and without relying on a signal bar.

Why We Created PetKeeper

As pet parents ourselves, we ran into the same problem with every existing app: they need constant high-speed internet, bury critical records behind slow cloud-only portals, and quietly ship ad SDKs that turn a medical record into ad inventory.

When you're in a clinic basement with no signal, or your dog needs a med dosed at 3am, you need the vaccination lot number, the allergy list, and the emergency contact right now — not after a loading spinner. PetKeeper is engineered offline-first, private, and fast, from the database up.

Our philosophy: your pet's medical data belongs to you. We don't sell it, we don't run ad trackers against it, and we never gate your own records behind a subscription wall.

Core Engineering Principles

1. Offline Independence, by Default

Every pet profile, reminder, expense, and medical event is written to a local SQLite database with native C++ bindings first. Search, filtering, and edits happen instantly — no network round-trip, ever, for anything you've already saved.

2. Strict Multi-Tenant Isolation

When you turn on sync, structured data goes to Cloud Firestore and media goes to a private Cloudflare R2 vault — each isolated by your Firebase Auth UID, enforced by production security rules, not just app-level checks.

3. Zero Advertising, Zero Telemetry

PetKeeper contains no AdMob, no behavioral trackers, and no analytics SDKs of any kind. We make money from the app itself, not from watching what you do inside it.

4. Ready for the Moments That Matter

From an instant zero-network emergency card to a one-tap vet summary PDF and a sitter handoff sheet, every export exists because a real pet parent needed it in a real hurry.
