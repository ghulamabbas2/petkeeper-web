Privacy by Architecture

Your pet's medical data isn't a product

No feed, no comments, no public profile — every record is private to your account, enforced at the database level.

🔒

Strict Per-User Isolation

Every request is checked against your signed-in account before any record is read or written — no record is ever reachable by another account.

📡

No Analytics or Data-Broker SDKs

Zero analytics trackers, zero Facebook SDK, zero data-broker libraries. Your pet's medical data is never sold or shared with third parties.

🗝️

Minimal Google Drive Access

Google Drive backup only asks for access to one hidden, private folder — it can never see or touch your other Drive files.

📴

Media Stays on Your Device by Default

Photos and documents live in a private local sandbox until you explicitly turn on cloud backup — the app tells you plainly when that's the case.

🧹

Full Account Deletion

One confirmed action permanently erases your cloud records, your stored photos and documents, and everything saved on your device, and disconnects your Google account — not just hidden.

📴

Works With Zero Connectivity

Every screen reads from your device first. A dead cell signal at the vet clinic never locks you out of your pet's records.
