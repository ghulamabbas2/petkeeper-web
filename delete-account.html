🗑️ Account Deletion

Delete Your PetKeeper Account

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time — permanently, and without needing to reach support first.

Option 1 — Delete In-App (Instant)

The fastest way to delete your account is directly inside PetKeeper:

1
Open Settings

Tap the Settings tab from the bottom navigation.

2
Go to Account & Danger Zone

Scroll to the "Danger Zone" section at the bottom of Settings.

3
Tap "Delete Account"

Confirm twice — this is deliberately a 2-step confirmation to prevent accidental deletion.

4
Done

Your local SQLite records, Firestore cloud documents, and Cloudflare R2 media files are deleted immediately.

Data categoryWhat happens
Account (Firebase UID, email, Google Sign-In link)Permanently deleted from Firebase Authentication within 30 days
Pet profiles & medical recordsRemoved from Cloud Firestore immediately
Photos & documentsPurged from Cloudflare R2 storage immediately
Local device dataDeleted immediately on confirmation, and again on app uninstall

Zero data retained once deletion is verified and fulfilled — no backup snapshots, no analytics rows, nothing held back.

Option 2 — Request Deletion From the Web

Lost access to the app, or want to request deletion without opening it? Fill out the form below — it goes straight to our support team. In-app deletion is instant; web requests are verified and completed within 30 days (typically much sooner).
