Account & Data Deletion Request

We respect your privacy and data ownership. You have full control to permanently delete your PetKeeper account, pet profiles, and all associated cloud files.

Application: PetKeeper (com.forneex.petkeeper) • Developer: Forneex

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you currently have the PetKeeper app installed on your Android device, you can permanently delete your account and all data in real-time:

1

Open Settings

Launch PetKeeper and tap the Settings gear icon on the top right bar or profile screen.

2

Account & Privacy

Scroll down to the Account & Danger Zone section and tap "Delete Account".

3

Confirm Purge

Confirm the deletion prompt. Your local SQLite records, Firestore cloud documents, and Cloudflare R2 media files are deleted immediately.

Method 2: Web-Based Account Deletion Request

If you have already uninstalled the application, lost access to your device, or prefer to submit a request via web, please complete the form below. Our data compliance team will verify and permanently purge your records within 30 days.

Data Categories Subject to Deletion

In compliance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy, here is the transparent breakdown of data handling upon deletion request:

Data Category Data Elements Deletion Handling & Retention
Account Identification Firebase UID, Email address, Google Sign-In profile link. Permanently Deleted from Firebase Authentication within 30 days.
Pet Health & Medical Records Pet profiles, microchip IDs, vaccination records, medications, allergies, weight logs, event timeline. Permanently Deleted from Cloud Firestore subcollections (/users/{userId}/...).
Documents & Vault Media Prescription scans, vaccination certificates, lab results, pet photos, receipts. Permanently Deleted from Cloudflare R2 object storage.
Local Device Data SQLite database on phone, offline media cache. Purged Immediately upon in-app deletion or upon uninstalling the app.
Retained Data None Zero data retained after deletion verification is fulfilled.

Processing Timeline

  • In-App Deletion: Instant (0 - 60 seconds).
  • Web Form / Email Submission: Handled by developer operations within 7 business days (maximum 30 days).
