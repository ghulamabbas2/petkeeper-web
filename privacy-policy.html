Privacy Policy

PetKeeper is designed with a strict offline-first, privacy-respecting architecture. Learn how your data is handled, stored, and protected.

Effective Date: August 18, 2026 • Application ID: com.forneex.petkeeper

1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Forneex ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the PetKeeper mobile application for Android (Package ID: com.forneex.petkeeper, hereinafter referred to as the "App").

We are committed to user privacy and data minimization. PetKeeper functions primarily as an offline-first local database. We do not sell your personal data, we do not engage in cross-app tracking, and we do not incorporate third-party advertising SDKs.

Core Privacy Guarantee: PetKeeper does not monetize through targeted ads or data harvesting. Your pet records, health histories, and document uploads remain your private property.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we process the following categories of information:

A. Account & Authentication Information

  • Firebase Auth Account Data: When you sign in (e.g., via Google Sign-In or email authentication), we collect your unique Firebase Authentication User ID (UID) and email address. This is used solely to authenticate your identity, secure your data, and enable cloud synchronization.

B. Pet Profile & Medical Data (User-Created)

  • Pet Identification: Pet name, species, breed, sex, neuter/spay status, birth date, microchip number (ISO), adoption date, and pet profile photos.
  • Medical Timeline & Logs: Vaccination records, medication schedules, dosage, veterinary clinic contacts, allergies, blood type, weight entries, and general care notes.
  • Reminders & Tasks: Booster schedules, flea/tick prevention intervals, grooming appointments, and custom reminders.
  • Pet Expenses: Self-entered financial entries (category, amount, merchant, date) for pet care budgeting.

C. User Documents & Files

  • Medical Vault Files: Scanned veterinary prescriptions, vaccination certificates, lab test results, and invoices uploaded by you from your device gallery or camera.

D. Device Information & Permissions

  • Camera & Media Storage: Used strictly when you choose to take a photo of your pet or attach a medical document from your device storage.
  • Notifications: Used by the local notification system (Notifee) to deliver scheduled booster, medication, and care reminders on your device.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your data exclusively for the following functional purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and operate the core features of the PetKeeper offline pet passport and care timeline.
  • To schedule and deliver offline notifications and alerts for upcoming pet medications and vaccinations.
  • To generate client-side PDF summaries (such as the Caregiver Handoff Summary and Emergency Vet Card).
  • To synchronize your structured data and documents across your authorized devices when cloud sync is enabled.
  • To enforce per-user storage quota limits (100 MB free storage allocation) and prevent abuse.

4. Data Storage & Multi-Tenant Architecture

PetKeeper utilizes a multi-layered security architecture:

Storage Layer Technology Privacy & Security Control
On-Device Local Cache SQLite (`op-sqlite` C++ JSI) Stored in Android's isolated internal app sandbox (/data/user/0/com.forneex.petkeeper/). Protected with .nomedia flags to prevent system gallery indexers from exposing medical records.
Cloud Structured Sync Google Cloud Firestore Strict multi-tenant security rules under /users/{userId}/.... Only the authenticated Firebase UID matching request.auth.uid can read, create, or delete records.
Cloud Document Vault Cloudflare R2 Media Vault Zero public URLs. Access is gated by a Cloudflare Worker that cryptographically validates your Firebase Auth JWT before granting ephemeral access.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with a minimal set of trusted infrastructure providers to power core functionality:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): Used for secure authentication (Firebase Auth) and structured cloud database synchronization (Cloud Firestore). Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare R2 (Cloudflare, Inc.): Used for private, encrypted media and document storage. Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Zero Third-Party Trackers: PetKeeper contains NO Google AdMob, NO Facebook SDK, NO Firebase Analytics, and NO marketing data broker libraries.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion Policy

We retain your data only for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide you with the application services.

  • On-Device Data: You can clear your local database at any time by clearing the application storage via Android Settings or tapping "Reset Local Data" in App Settings.
  • Account Deletion (Google Play Compliance): You have the right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated cloud data at any time.

You can initiate account deletion either:

  1. Directly In-App: Navigate to Settings → Account → Delete Account. This immediately deletes your local database, purges your Firestore cloud records, and deletes your Cloudflare R2 media files.
  2. Via Public Web Form: Visit our dedicated Account Deletion Request Page without needing the app installed. All deletion requests submitted via the web are verified and fully purged within 30 days.

7. Data Security Measures

We implement technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal and pet information against unauthorized access, destruction, or alteration:

  • All network communication with Firebase and Cloudflare is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Cloudflare R2 media tokens expire after short durations and cannot be forged or accessed publicly.
  • Local device storage is strictly sandboxed by Android operating system permissions.

8. Children's Privacy

PetKeeper is not directed to individuals under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal data without parental consent, we will take immediate steps to delete such information.

9. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

Depending on your geographic jurisdiction, you have specific legal rights regarding your data:

  • Right of Access: You can view all records directly in the app or request an export.
  • Right to Rectification: You can modify your pet records and profile information directly at any time.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): You can permanently delete your account and all data via the app or our Account Deletion Page.
  • Right to Data Portability: You can export your data to formatted PDF summaries (Emergency Card, Caregiver Handoff).

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements in the App or regulatory updates. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated Effective Date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
